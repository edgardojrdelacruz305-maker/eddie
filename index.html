Enrollment process

What to expect from application to confirmed enrollment.

👤

1. Create Account

Register with a valid email address and a password you'll remember.

📝

2. Student Information

Enter your personal details exactly as they appear on your documents.

🌾

3. Select Program

Pick one program. You can change this before you submit.

📎

4. Upload Requirements

Clear photos or scans — birth certificate, Form 138, 2x2 photo, valid ID.

🔎

5. Review Application

Look over your entries and documents one more time.

✅

6. Submit Enrollment

You'll get an application number and a status you can track.

Requirements checklist

Have these ready before you start the upload step.

📄

Birth Certificate

PSA-issued copy, or a clear scan of your local civil registry copy.

🗂️

Report Card / Form 138

From your most recently completed school year.

📷

2x2 Photo

Recent, white background, clear face — same as for your school ID.

🪪

Valid ID

Any government-issued ID, or a school ID with your photo.

📌

Other Documents

Good Moral Certificate, honorable dismissal, or other documents your program may ask for.

Tawi-Tawi Regional Agricultural College

Based in Bongao, Tawi-Tawi, TRAC has trained generations of students in agriculture, fisheries, and allied fields. AGRiENROLL brings the college's enrollment process online, so applicants across the province can apply without traveling to campus first.
